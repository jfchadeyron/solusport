# Identité visuelle de SoluSport

Le logo SoluSport porte la signature « Votre sport, nos solutions ». Les quatre identités produits sont celles fournies par le porteur du projet. Les versions web conservent leurs proportions, leur fond et leurs couleurs.

| Fichier | Identité et usage |
| --- | --- |
| `assets/logos/solusport.webp` | Marque SoluSport, en-tête et pied de page |
| `assets/logos/presensport.webp` | PresenSport, présences et uniformes |
| `assets/logos/gestionsport.webp` | GestionSport, administration et communication |
| `assets/logos/notasport.webp` | NotaSport, organisation de compétitions et notation |
| `assets/logos/equipsport.webp` | EquipSport, tenues et équipements sportifs sur mesure |

La palette d’interface associe le bleu marine `#082945`, l’orange `#FF812F`, le fond ivoire `#F6F4EF` et le bleu clair `#EEF4F7`. La police Manrope est auto-hébergée avec sa licence dans le dossier des ressources.

Les photographies d’illustration ont été créées avec le générateur Imagegen intégré, puis optimisées en WebP. Elles représentent des situations fictives et des pistes de personnalisation. Les caractéristiques des équipements se précisent pour chaque projet.

## Prompts des quatre nouvelles illustrations

### Entraînement

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create an original premium editorial photograph for the homepage of the French sports association brand SoluSport. Extra-wide landscape banner, 2.7:1 composition. A real-life moment in a bright French municipal multi-sport gym with warm oak floor and painted court markings, daylight from high windows, tactile documentary film quality. Put the main subjects in the RIGHTMOST 45% of the composition: a female club coach with dark tied-back hair in a navy athletic polo, three-quarter back and side view, holding a clipboard at her waist and engaged in conversation with two young adult athletes in off-white and orange training tees. Mid-distance, natural proportions, candid expressions, quietly purposeful. The LEFT 50% shows only shadowed out-of-focus dark navy gym wall and court edge with restrained ambient texture, available for text overlay. No screen UI, no drawn graphics, no logos, no letters, no readable text, no watermark. Frame faces in upper middle of the right side and do not cut off heads. Professional European sports campaign photography, daylight, believable faces and hands.

### Vie associative

Photorealistic editorial campaign photo, horizontal 3:2. Inside the welcoming clubhouse of a French amateur sports association on a season registration evening, a woman volunteer in a navy overshirt and a second adult discussing the season side by side at a wooden desk with a simple laptop, ring binder and small blank paper calendar. In the soft background a sports hall and blue/orange workout kit hanging on a rail suggest the club context. Composition focuses on people working together, centered subjects, authentic documentary feeling, natural window light, restrained navy, ivory and terracotta accents, contemporary, no legible words, no logos, no watermark, no promotional graphics, no handshake.

### Compétition

Editorial sports documentary photography, premium and believable for a French sports association website header. Wide 3:2 landscape at the judges' table of an amateur indoor gymnastics competition. Two adult judges in navy polos shown in three-quarter profile looking toward the floor, foreground an off-white score sheet and a tablet, blurred gymnasts and orange court accents in the distance. Restrained deep navy, warm wood and natural daylight, candid attentive expressions, realistic sports venue, clean contemporary framing with primary subjects centered. No legible text, no scores, no logos or watermarks.

### Tenues et équipements

Use case: product-mockup. Original editorial studio product photograph for EquipSport: a coordinated bespoke team kit for a French sports association. Landscape 3:2. On a low oak bench beside the softly blurred painted floor of a sports hall, show a bespoke dark navy sports duffel and a well-constructed larger custom rectangular transport case for club training equipment, with strong orange piping, webbing handles, quality zips and stitched reinforcing panels. Beside it an orange-and-ivory technical jersey draped tidily over the edge, a small matching kit pouch, and a few training bibs. The equipment and textile case must be at least as prominent as the jersey. Composition visually balanced, all main items visible, clean professional catalogs, natural diffuse side light, crisp fabric, subtle grain, restrained ivory navy and orange palette. An illustrative product concept, no logos, no brand, no typography, no numbers, no watermarks, no people. No sale labels or UI.
